Safer gambling

18+ only (21+ where local law requires). Gambling involves risk of loss. Free, confidential help:

  • US National Helpline: call or text 1-800-GAMBLER (ncpgambling.org)
  • GamCare (UK and international)
  • BeGambleAware (UK and international)
  • Gamban (blocks gambling sites & apps across devices)
