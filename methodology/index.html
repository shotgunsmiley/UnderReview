What each entry can show

  • Licence and number, compared with the regulator's own public register where that register could be read. So what: a licence seal on a website is not proof; the register is.
  • Operating company named in the casino's terms. So what: this is the business you would have a dispute with.
  • Custody: who holds your funds while you play. So what: with a custodial balance, the casino controls the withdrawal.
  • Coins and networks the casino lists. So what: sending a coin on an unsupported network can lose it, and network fees are separate from any casino fee.
  • KYC trigger wording in the terms. So what: "no-KYC" sign-up can still mean ID checks later.
  • Withdrawal and turnover rules, bonus terms and restricted countries. So what: these rules decide whether and when you can cash out.
  • Responsible-gambling tools and support channels the casino lists.

The four status definitions

Checked
We read the regulator's public register and confirmed the licence, not that the casino's site says so. A register page may show a status such as "Assessment in progress". We show the status as written, and a page may have changed since it was read.
Stated
Taken from the casino's own terms or help pages. The source is linked. This is what the casino says, not independent proof.
Unverified
Written without a recorded source. Treat it as a lead to check, not a fact.
Unknown
We have no data, or we looked and did not find it. It never means "none" or "no limit".

Entry status lines

  • "Partly checked": the entry has at least one Checked field.
  • "Casino-stated only": the entry has sources, all from the casino's own pages.
  • "Unverified": the entry has no recorded sources.

The line describes source coverage. It is not a grade, and entries are never sorted by it.

Which fields appear first

A field appears in the At a Glance panel only when at least half of the entries have some text for it. Other fields are listed under All fields. Every value carries a badge that says whether it was checked on a regulator's register, stated on the casino's own page, or has no recorded source.

Re-check schedule

Re-check schedule: not yet set. There is no fixed re-check cycle yet. An entry's "Last verified" field shows the date its sources were read. Source labels give the page snapshot date where one is known.

What unranked means

No scores, stars, "best for" labels, top picks or featured slots. Entries are grouped by category and listed A–Z, which is not a quality order. The compare table shows entries in the order you pick them.

One exception: the opinion piece The Glass Vault and the Company Safe gives the author's own scores out of 10. They are labelled as opinion on that page, are not verification statuses, and never affect the directory or its order.

Links and money

The directory contains no affiliate links. Some entries list "Sources" links to a regulator's public register or to the casino's own terms or help pages. These are plain reference links, not affiliate links, and earn nothing. If affiliate links are ever added, each one will be labelled "Affiliate link" next to it and will not change the order or wording of entries.

Common questions

What does "unranked" mean here?

No entry is scored, starred, ranked, featured or labelled "best for". Entries are grouped by what they do and listed A–Z, which says nothing about quality. Being listed is not a recommendation or a safety rating. See What unranked means.

Does "no-KYC" mean a casino will never ask for ID?

Not necessarily. "No-KYC" usually describes sign-up. A casino's terms can still allow identity or source-of-funds checks later, for example before a withdrawal or after an automated risk flag. Read the verification section of the terms before you deposit. Our KYC field shows wording from the casino's terms only where a source is linked; otherwise it is labelled Unverified or Unknown.

What does "provably fair" mean?

The casino commits to a secret server seed by publishing its hash before you bet. Afterwards it reveals the seed, so you can recompute the result from the server seed, your client seed and the nonce. That proves the result was not changed after the bet. It does not prove the house edge, the licence, or that you will be paid. Read more in the provably fair guide.

How do I check a casino's licence on a regulator's register?

(1) Find the licence number and the licensed company name in the casino's footer or terms. (2) Go to the regulator's official website yourself; do not rely on the seal link in the casino's footer. (3) Search the public register by licence number or company name. (4) Check that the company, the website domain and the licence status match. If you cannot find a match, treat the licence claim as unverified.

Why do so many fields say "Unknown"?

A field says Unknown when we have no text for it. Missing data is shown as unknown rather than guessed. Unknown never means "none" or "no limit".

Is a casino in this directory legal where I live?

This site cannot tell you that. Laws differ by country and region. Check your local law and the casino's restricted-countries list. This site is educational, not legal advice.
