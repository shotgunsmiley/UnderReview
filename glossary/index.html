Glossary

GGR (Gross Gaming Revenue)

Total money wagered by players minus total winnings paid back out—before operator taxes or marketing costs.

House edge

The theoretical percentage of all wagered money a game keeps over millions of rounds. It is what is left after RTP: a game with 99% RTP has a 1% house edge. A provably fair check does not prove the house edge.

KYC / AML & Zero-Knowledge KYC

Know Your Customer & Anti-Money Laundering checks. ZK-KYC lets a user prove they are over 18 and in an approved country without handing over passport scans to every casino.

Liquidity Pool (On-Chain Bankroll)

A smart contract vault where users deposit stablecoins to act as the house bankroll, earning a share of house edge while bearing variance risk.

Nonce

"Number used once." A counter that increases by +1 with every bet you place so the same seed pair generates a fresh outcome each round.

Provably fair

The casino commits to a secret server seed by publishing its hash before you bet. Afterwards it reveals the seed, so you can recompute the result from the server seed, your client seed and the nonce. That shows the result was not changed after the bet. It does not prove the house edge, the licence, or that you will be paid.

RTP (Return to Player)

The theoretical percentage of all wagered money a game pays back over millions of rounds (e.g., 99% RTP = 1% house edge).

SHA-256 Hash

A one-way digital fingerprint. Changing even a single letter in the original secret seed completely alters the resulting 64-character hash.
