Non-Custodial vs. Platform Wallets

"Non-custodial" protocols (such as Dexsport) keep funds in your personal Web3 wallet until a smart contract executes the wager. Most other platforms use custodial account balances where you deposit into a platform-controlled wallet before playing.

Most Web3 casinos operate under Curaçao or Anjouan licenses—crypto-friendly jurisdictions that offer lighter regulatory oversight and dispute recourse than Tier-1 regulators like the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) or UKGC.

Pillar 02 // Cryptographic Trust

Provably Fair Technology Explained

In traditional online casinos, you have to trust that the company’s server didn’t rig the spin. Provably Fair uses cryptographic hashing (like a sealed envelope) so neither the casino nor the player can change the outcome after a bet is placed.

1

Server Seed Hash Locked

Before you bet, the casino picks a secret random number (Server Seed) and shows you its scrambled fingerprint (SHA-256 Hash). Like putting the answer inside a locked glass box before the game starts.

2

Your Player Seed Added

Your browser adds your own random text (Client Seed) plus your bet counter (Nonce). Because the casino couldn’t predict your seed when it locked Step 1, it cannot pre-rig the result.

3

Outcome Revealed & Verified

After the round, the casino reveals the unscrambled Server Seed. Any independent tool (including your browser right below) can re-run the SHA-256 math to confirm it matches Step 1’s fingerprint.

Pillar 04 // Decentralized Architecture

Smart Contract & On-Chain Casinos

In a non-custodial smart contract casino, you never deposit money into a company’s bank account. Instead, your wallet interacts directly with open-source code holding a shared liquidity pool.

STEP A // WALLET

Direct Bet Signature

Funds stay in your wallet until the exact second a wager executes.

STEP B // CONTRACT

Liquidity Pool Escrow

Community-funded bankroll guarantees solvency visibly on-chain.

STEP C // PAYOUT

Atomic Settlement

Winning payouts transfer automatically in the same block—no manual approval.

CRITICAL RISK AUDIT Read Before Using

3 Key Risks of On-Chain Casinos

  • 1. Smart Contract Exploits If the underlying code has an unaudited bug, hackers can drain the shared liquidity pool—and blockchain transactions cannot be reversed.
  • 2. Randomness & Oracle Manipulation Poorly designed contracts that rely on block timestamps instead of verifiable randomness (VRF) can be gamed by validators or bots.
  • 3. Liquidity Provider Drawdowns Users who stake funds to "be the house" face real financial loss during whale winning streaks or token price drops.

Pillar 07 // Threat Intelligence

Scam & Fraud Pattern Library

Fraudulent operators recycle the exact same playbooks under new brand names. Learn the four most common recurring traps before depositing a single dollar.

TYPOLOGY 01 REGULATORY SPOOFING

Fake Footer License Seals

How It Works: Scam sites paste static PNG images of Curaçao, MGA, or UKGC badges in their footer without an active validator link.

How to Spot It: Click the seal. Genuine licenses open an official regulator certificate page matching the exact domain name.
TYPOLOGY 02 SELECTIVE KYC

Withdrawal-Trap KYC & "Tax" Fees

How It Works: A site lets you deposit and "win" huge balances effortlessly, then demands an upfront "verification deposit" or "unlock tax" before releasing funds.

How to Spot It: Legitimate casinos never require you to deposit extra money to withdraw your winnings.
TYPOLOGY 03 PONZI TOKENOMICS

"Guaranteed Yield / AI Arbitrage" Betting Pools

How It Works: Promoters claim an "AI sports-betting bot" generates 1%–3% guaranteed daily returns if you stake tokens into their house pool.

How to Spot It: No betting algorithm eliminates variance. Fixed daily yields are paid from new deposits until the pool collapses.
TYPOLOGY 04 MALICIOUS SIGNATURES

Fake Airdrop & "Free Spin" Wallet Drainers

How It Works: Social media replies offer "free VIP bonus chips" that prompt your wallet to sign an opaque Permit2 or setApprovalForAll transaction.

How to Spot It: Claiming a legitimate bonus never requires granting token-transfer permissions on your existing wallet balances.

Plain-English Glossary

GGR (Gross Gaming Revenue) INDUSTRY METRIC

Total money wagered by players minus total winnings paid back out—before operator taxes or marketing costs.

SHA-256 Hash CRYPTOGRAPHY

A one-way digital fingerprint. Changing even a single letter in the original secret seed completely alters the resulting 64-character hash.

Nonce PROVABLY FAIR

"Number used once." A counter that increases by +1 with every bet you place so the same seed pair generates a fresh outcome each round.

Liquidity Pool (On-Chain Bankroll) DEFI / SMART CONTRACTS

A smart contract vault where users deposit stablecoins to act as the house bankroll, earning a share of house edge while bearing variance risk.

KYC / AML & Zero-Knowledge KYC COMPLIANCE

Know Your Customer & Anti-Money Laundering checks. ZK-KYC lets a user prove they are over 18 and in an approved country without handing over passport scans to every casino.

RTP (Return to Player) GAME MATH

The theoretical percentage of all wagered money a game pays back over millions of rounds (e.g., 99% RTP = 1% house edge).

Methodology // Plain English

How We Review

This page explains what each directory entry can show and how to read it. It describes only what the site displays today: fields, sources and dates.

What each entry can show

A field is only filled in when a source has been read. Fields without a source are marked Unverified or Unknown.

  • Licence and number, compared with the regulator's own public register where that register could be read. So what: a licence seal on a website is not proof; the register is.
  • Operating company named in the casino's terms. So what: this is the business you would have a dispute with.
  • Custody: who holds your funds while you play. So what: with a custodial balance, the casino controls the withdrawal.
  • Coins and networks the casino lists. So what: sending a coin on an unsupported network can lose it, and network fees are separate from any casino fee.
  • KYC trigger wording in the terms. So what: "no-KYC" sign-up can still mean ID checks later.
  • Withdrawal and turnover rules, bonus terms and restricted countries. So what: these rules decide whether and when you can cash out.
  • Responsible-gambling tools and support channels the casino lists.

Stated vs checked

Checked: confirmed on an independent primary source, such as the regulator's register. The source is linked. A register page may show a status such as "Assessment in progress". We show the status as written, and a page may have changed since it was read.

Stated by the casino: taken from the casino's own terms or help pages. The source is linked. This is what the casino says, not independent proof.

Unverified: written without a recorded source. Treat it as a lead to check, not a fact.

Unknown (not yet checked): we have no data. It never means "none" or "no limit".

Questions // Plain English

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "unranked" mean here?

No entry is scored, starred, ranked, featured or labelled "best for". Entries are grouped by what they do and listed in catalog order, which says nothing about quality. Being listed is not a recommendation or a safety rating. See How we review.

Does "no-KYC" mean a casino will never ask for ID?

Not necessarily. "No-KYC" usually describes sign-up. A casino's terms can still allow identity or source-of-funds checks later, for example before a withdrawal or after an automated risk flag. Read the verification section of the terms before you deposit. Our KYC field shows wording from the casino's terms only where a source is linked; otherwise it is labelled Unverified or Unknown.

What does "provably fair" mean?

The casino commits to a secret server seed by publishing its hash before you bet. Afterwards it reveals the seed, so you can recompute the result from the server seed, your client seed and the nonce. That proves the result was not changed after the bet. It does not prove the house edge, the licence, or that you will be paid. Try it in the provably fair lab.

How do I check a casino's licence on a regulator's register?

(1) Find the licence number and the licensed company name in the casino's footer or terms. (2) Go to the regulator's official website yourself; do not rely on the seal link in the casino's footer. (3) Search the public register by licence number or company name. (4) Check that the company, the website domain and the licence status match. If you cannot find a match, treat the licence claim as unverified.

Why do so many fields say "Unknown (not yet checked)"?

We only fill in a field from a source we have actually read. Missing data is shown as unknown rather than guessed. Unknown never means "none" or "no limit".

Is a casino in this directory legal where I live?

This site cannot tell you that. Laws differ by country and region. Check your local law and the casino's restricted-countries list. This site is educational, not legal advice.
